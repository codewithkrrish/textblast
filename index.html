Good to know

    
    
      
  • The preview only renders the first 2,000 characters. With 100,000 repeats the full output can be millions of characters long, and rendering all of it would freeze the page. Copy and Download always use the complete output, even when the preview is truncated.
    • 
      
  • Huge outputs can be slow to copy. A 100,000-repeat job with a long text can be several megabytes; the clipboard may take a few seconds, and some apps paste large text slowly. Downloading the .txt file is the reliable route for very large outputs.
    • 
      
  • Your settings are remembered, your text is not. The repeat count, separator, numbering, prefix and suffix are saved in this browser (localStorage). The text itself is never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere.
    • 
      
  • Counts are clamped to 1–100,000. Typing 0, a negative number or anything over 100,000 snaps back into range instead of erroring.
    • 
      
  • Numbers count as one word each. The word count treats "1." or "[42]" as a single word, the same way it treats any whitespace-separated token.
